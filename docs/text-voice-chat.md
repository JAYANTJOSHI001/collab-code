# Voice Chat and Text Messaging

**Collab** isn't just about coding together — it's about seamless communication while doing it. Whether you're debugging complex merge conflicts, brainstorming architecture, or pair programming, Collab supports both **voice chat** and **text chat** for true real-time collaboration.

---

### 🎙️ Voice Chat (WebRTC-Based)

Collab uses **WebRTC** to establish peer-to-peer voice communication between collaborators. This lets you speak with your team as if you're in the same room — no third-party meeting tools needed.

To start a voice chat:

- 🔹Open the **Collaborators panel** (usually on the right-hand side).
- 🔹Click the **microphone icon** beside your name or in the panel header.
- 🔹Voice chat will start automatically with other active users in the room.

> 🔐 Note: Your browser may ask for microphone access permission. Make sure to allow it for a smooth experience.
> 

---

### 💬 Text Chat (Side Messaging Panel)

For quick notes, links, or asynchronous messages, the built-in text chat panel is ideal.

To send a text message:

- 🔹On the **left sidebar**, find the **chat bubble icon** (above the Files panel).
- 🔹Click it to open the **messaging panel** on the right-hand side.
- 🔹Type your message and press **Enter** to send it to all active collaborators in the room.

This feature helps you keep track of ideas, share context, and communicate quietly when working across different time zones.
